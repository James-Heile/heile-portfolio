# Skill: Resume Tailoring

## Overview
This skill guides the AI to analyze a provided job description (JD) alongside my master resume and tailor bullet points to highlight relevant technical, analytical, and professional experience.

## Objectives
1. Extract key hard skills, soft skills, and domain keywords from the target job description.
2. Align candidate experience with job requirements while maintaining strict factual accuracy.
3. Rewrite resume bullet points using dynamic action verbs and quantified impact (metrics, tools, results).

## Inputs Required
- **Target Job Description:** Plain text or link to the position posting.
- **Master Resume:** Standard experience details (from `RESUME.md`).

## Processing Rules & Guidelines
- **Factual Integrity:** Never invent roles, certifications, companies, or fake metrics.
- **Style & Structure:**
  - Use strong lead action verbs (e.g., *Engineered*, *Optimized*, *Formulated*, *Analyzed*).
  - Structure bullets using: **Action Verb + Context/Tool + Measurable Result**.
  - Avoid passive language ("Responsible for", "Helped with").
- **Tone:** Professional, precise, and concise.

## Expected Output Format
Deliver the response formatted in clean Markdown with three clear sections:

### 1. Key Keyword Alignment
- List 3–5 core skills/keywords extracted from the job description and how they map to my background.

### 2. Tailored Experience Bullets
- Bullet points updated specifically for the target role.

### 3. Gap Analysis & Quick Fixes
- Brief recommendations on any key qualifications missing from the resume that could be addressed in an interview or cover letter.
